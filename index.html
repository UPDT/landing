FAQ

Common questions

How much does development cost?

We name the price after a free estimate, usually within 2 business days. It is fixed in the contract, and you pay by stage after each demo.

Can we start without a spec?

Yes. We break down the task on the first call, then write the spec ourselves and agree it with you.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code lives in your repository from day one, and the rights transfer under the contract.

Do you take over projects from other contractors?

Yes. We review the code in 3–5 days, then send a plan of changes with dates.

What happens after launch?

We keep working under a support contract. Bugs are fixed within 24 hours, and new versions ship every two weeks.

Do you work under a contract?

Yes, with companies and individuals alike. You get an invoice after every stage.
